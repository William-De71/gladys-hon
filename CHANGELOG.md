## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-14

### Added

- Connection to the hOn cloud with the e-mail address and the password of the hOn app account (Haier, Candy, Hoover), through the CIAM login of the app; the session is kept in `/data` and reused after a restart, and a refused login pauses the next attempts instead of locking the account.
- Every appliance of the account offered in the Discover tab, its features read from what the appliance reports: washing machines, washer dryers and tumble dryers (state, phase, program, time left, pause, door, remote control, energy and water consumption, error), dishwashers (with salt and rinse aid to refill), air conditioners (on/off, mode, setpoint, fan speed, swing, indoor and outdoor temperatures), fridges, freezers and wine cellars (zone temperatures and setpoints, doors, super cool, super freeze, holiday and auto modes, room temperature and humidity), ovens (state, temperatures, time left) and the other types (state, temperature, error).
- Programs started from Gladys (program selection of washers, dryers and dishwashers), cycles paused and resumed, air conditioners and fridge setpoints controlled, when remote control is enabled on the appliance.
- Statuses read every 30 s to 5 min, published within the Gladys rate limit, with a cloud or unreachable badge per appliance.
- Dashboard widgets "hOn appliances" (every appliance: state, phase, time left) and "hOn appliance" (one appliance: progress, temperatures, pause, resume, stop and on/off buttons).
- Scene triggers: a cycle starts, a cycle is finished, an appliance reports an error, an appliance needs a refill, an appliance goes offline or comes back online, the hOn account is refused.
- Scene actions: start a program (with an optional delayed start), pause, resume or stop a cycle, read the state of an appliance.
- Configuration buttons "Test the connection" and "List the programs".
- Texts in English, French, German and Spanish.
